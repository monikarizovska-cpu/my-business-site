Meet Monika

Monika Rizovska Tanevska is the Founder of DAVLEO, an AI transformation consultancy that helps SMEs and growing businesses identify AI opportunities, redesign business processes, and implement practical AI solutions.

Through business diagnostics, AI transformation strategy, and AI-powered workflows and agents, DAVLEO helps businesses turn AI from a collection of tools into a practical part of how their teams work.

Monika is Global Community Director across 74 countries at Women in Tech Global and Director of Women in Tech Macedonia. She is an AI evangelist, international speaker, mentor, startup competition judge, and investor, with extensive experience across technology, innovation, entrepreneurship, and community building.

She co-founded Ways2Help, a volunteer organization supporting socially vulnerable families, and Talentino, an educational center focused on STEM and AI PlayLab. She is a two-time Startup Weekend winner and has received Women in Tech Macedonia awards for community impact, individual impact, and philanthropy through Ways2Help.

As the leader of AI Collective Skopje, Monika works at the intersection of AI, business transformation, human capital, and innovation, helping organizations and communities prepare for the future of work.
